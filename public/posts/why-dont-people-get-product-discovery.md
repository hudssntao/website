Starting two weeks ago, I've been on a mission to make operating restaurants easier for owners. Naturally, the first step of that process is _product discovery_, "an iterative process where teams deeply understand user needs and business context to identify and validate product opportunities before building".

What does that look like in practice?

Market research to capture the big picture, and many, many, many interviews to solidfy a specific problem statement and persona. 

As I was doing this though, I begin to notice a pattern. _People just don't get it_ (at least not the first time).
